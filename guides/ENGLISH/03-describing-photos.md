# Describing photos

## How to compare two photos
Talk about **what you see**, the **similarities**, the **differences** and **what you can guess** (who, where, why, how they feel).

:::key
**Useful language: describing a photo**
- **Contrast:** *The first photo shows … **whereas / while** the other one shows …*
- **Both:** ***Both** families are … · In **both** photos, the people …*
- **Guessing (speculating):**
  - *It **looks as if** / It **seems as if** they are celebrating …*
  - *They **seem** to be having a great time. / They **look / seem** quite happy.*
  - *The photo **must** have been taken in summer.* (you're almost sure)
  - *They **could** be celebrating … / The photo **might** have been taken in summer as they are wearing light clothes.* (possible)
  - ***Perhaps / Maybe** they are …*
- **Alternatives:** *inside a house **or** maybe a restaurant*
:::

## Model description (Exercise 1, completed)
a) The first photo shows a family outside **whereas/while** the other one shows a family inside a house **or** maybe a restaurant.
b) **Both** families are having something to eat. In the first photo, the people **must/might** be having a barbecue in the park. There is a man who is grilling the meal **while** the other people are waiting for the food to be ready.
c) As for the second photo, **perhaps** they are celebrating a wedding anniversary since they have a cake with candles on the table and a man is offering the couple some champagne. Behind the couple we can see an older man, who **might/must** be the boy's grandfather. Everyone is clapping their hands.
d) In both photos, the people **seem** to be enjoying themselves and look quite happy because they are smiling.
