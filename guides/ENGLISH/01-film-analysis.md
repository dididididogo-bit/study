# Film analysis: Enola Holmes

## The film at a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| **Main character** | Enola Holmes |
| **Other characters** | Sherlock Holmes, Mycroft Holmes, Viscount Tewkesbury |
| **Setting** | England, 19th century |
| **Enola's traits** | determined, intelligent, strong-willed, intrepid |
| **Genre** | adventure, crime, mystery |

## The story (what you need to remember)
- Enola grows up **alone** with her mother; her memories of her mother are **wonderful**: she was her whole world.
- When her mother disappears, Enola thinks **Sherlock Holmes will have all the answers**.
- When her brothers arrive, Mycroft is **shocked** because Enola has **no hat and gloves** (not "proper" for a young lady in the 19th century).
- **Mycroft** thinks she is a **wild child**; **Sherlock** says she **seems intelligent**.

:::key
**Message of the film:** we can **choose the way to live our lives**, or follow other people's opinions. Enola's name spelled backwards is *alone*: she learns to be independent.
:::

## Background facts
- **Sherlock Holmes** was a **fictional** detective (not a real person).
- He was created by **Sir Arthur Conan Doyle**.
