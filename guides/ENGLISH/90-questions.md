## Writing: emails
:::q Writing · Task · A friend is staying in your house while you're on holiday. Write an email (100–150 words): say where important things are, ask them to do something, suggest things to do in the area.
?? Model answer (informal):
*Hi Sam,*
*Thanks so much for looking after the house! The keys are under the plant pot by the front door and the wifi password is on the fridge. There's plenty of food in the cupboard next to the cooker, so help yourself.*
*Could you please water the plants in the garden every evening and feed Tiger, my cat? His food is in the blue box in the kitchen.*
*If you get bored, why don't you go to the beach? It's only ten minutes by bike. There's also a great pizza place in the square, and the cinema has cheap tickets on Tuesdays.*
*Have fun and see you in two weeks!*
*Love, Pedro*
Check: 3 points covered · informal greeting and ending · contractions · 100–150 words. (7 marks content, 6 marks style.)
:::
:::q Writing · p. 23 · Is "Dear Mr Davis … Kind regards, Harry Miller" formal or informal? Name 3 features.
?? **Formal**: title + surname in the greeting, **full forms** (I am, I would), formal vocabulary and closing remark (*I look forward to hearing from you*), ending *Kind regards* + full name.
:::
:::q Writing · p. 23 · Give an informal greeting, opening, closing remark and ending.
?? *Hi Helen / Dear Helen* · *Thanks for your email! / How are you?* · *Write soon! / Can't wait to see you!* · *Love, / Best wishes,* + first name.
:::

## Describing photos
:::q Photos · Ex 1 · Complete: "The first photo shows a family outside (1)___ the other one shows a family inside a house (2)___ maybe a restaurant."
?? (1) **whereas / while** · (2) **or**
:::
:::q Photos · Ex 1 · Complete: "(3)___ families are having something to eat. In the first photo, the people (4)___ be having a barbecue in the park. There is a man who is grilling the meal (5)___ the other people are waiting."
?? (3) **Both** · (4) **must / might** · (5) **while**
:::
:::q Photos · Ex 1 · Complete: "As for the second photo, (6)___ they are celebrating a wedding anniversary … an older man, who (7)___ be the boy's grandfather."
?? (6) **perhaps** · (7) **might / must**
:::
:::q Photos · Ex 1 · Complete: "In both photos, the people (8)___ to be enjoying themselves."
?? (8) **seem**
:::
:::q Photos · Ex 2 · Describe and compare the two photos (people sharing a cake; young people at an outdoor festival with confetti).
?? Model: *Both photos show people enjoying themselves together. The first one shows a few people sharing a cake at a table, **whereas** the second one shows a big group of young people outside. **It looks as if** the people in the first photo are celebrating a birthday. In the second photo they **could be** at a festival, because there's confetti in the air. **It must have been taken** in summer as they are wearing light clothes. In both photos they **seem** to be having a great time.*
:::

## Use of English (p. 32)

?? I don't like **swimming in the sea**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 1b · It's OK with me if we have a pizza. → I don't mind ___.
?? I don't mind **having a pizza**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 1c · I'm not going to eat chocolate every day. → I'm going to stop ___.
?? I'm going to stop **eating chocolate every day**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 1d · I'm pleased you came to see me. Thank you. → Thank you for ___.
?? Thank you for **coming to see me**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 2a · A: I think we should use dictionaries in exams. B: I 'm agreeing / agree with you.
?? **agree** (stative verb → present simple).
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 2b · A: Where's Orlando? B: He 's talking / talks to Mr Lin.
?? **'s talking** (happening now).
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 2c · Sorry, I 'm having / have swimming practice every Wednesday evening.
?? **have** (routine → present simple).
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 2d · It's OK. I 'm not taking / don't take sugar.
?? **don't take** (habit).
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 2e · Are there any questions about plans for this term? B: Do we go / Are we going on a school trip?
?? **Are we going** (future arrangement → present continuous).
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 3b · Rewrite in the present simple passive: My parents expect me to tidy my room every Saturday.
?? **I'm expected to tidy my room every Saturday.**
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 3c · Our school encourages us to play a musical instrument.
?? **We are encouraged to play a musical instrument.**
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 3d · We invite all parents to come to the end-of-term concert.
?? **All parents are invited to come to the end-of-term concert.**
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 4b · Why can't we go into the gym? (clean) → Because …
?? Because **it's being cleaned**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 4c · Why can't I use the computer? (repair)
?? Because **it's being repaired**.
:::
:::q Grammar · Ex 4d · Why are you taking those letters to the office? (send / to parents this afternoon)
?? Because **they're being sent to parents this afternoon**.
:::
