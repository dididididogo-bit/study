# The carbon cycle

**Key words:** carbon cycle

## Carbon and living organisms
- Carbon is an **element** (symbol **C**), a **non-metal**. It occurs naturally in different forms: **diamonds** and **graphite** (the "lead" in a pencil) are pure carbon.
- Living organisms **cannot use carbon as an element** — only when it is part of a **compound**.
- Carbon is in many compounds that make up cells: **carbohydrates, proteins and fats** all contain carbon.
- **Plants** take **carbon dioxide** from the air and use it in photosynthesis to make **carbohydrates**. Then they use carbohydrates to make proteins and fats.
- **Animals** get carbon compounds by **eating** plants or other animals. **Decomposers** get carbon by breaking down **waste products and dead** plants and animals.

## Returning carbon dioxide to the air — respiration
**glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water**
- **All organisms respire all the time** (plants, animals and decomposers). Respiration releases CO₂ back into the air.
- At **night**, plants cannot photosynthesise, so they only **give out** carbon dioxide.

## Fossil fuels and combustion
- When organisms die in places with **no oxygen** (e.g. deep in the ocean), decomposers cannot work. The bodies get buried; **high pressure and heat** over **millions of years** turn them into **fossil fuels**: **coal, oil and natural gas**.
  - **Coal** formed from plants in huge **swamps**.
  - **Oil and gas** formed from tiny **marine** organisms on the sea bed.
- **Combustion:** burning a fossil fuel makes the carbon combine with oxygen → **carbon dioxide** returns to the air.

:::key
**The carbon cycle — processes**
- **Photosynthesis:** CO₂ in the air → carbohydrates in plants.
- **Feeding:** plants → animals; plants and animals → decomposers.
- **Respiration:** plants, animals and decomposers → CO₂ in the air.
- **Very slow formation:** dead organisms → fossil fuels.
- **Combustion:** fossil fuels → CO₂ in the air.
:::

## Think like a scientist — how do plants and animals affect CO₂ concentration?
**Hydrogencarbonate indicator** changes colour with CO₂ concentration:
| Colour | CO₂ |
|---|---|
| **Purple** | **No** carbon dioxide |
| **Red** | **Low** concentration |
| **Yellow** | **Higher** concentration |
