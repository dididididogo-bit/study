# Plants and water

**Key words:** root hairs · xylem vessels

## Why living things need water
All living things need water to stay alive — for example, to **dissolve chemicals** inside their cells so that chemical reactions (such as **respiration**) can happen.

## How root hairs absorb water
- Plants are made mostly of water. They absorb water almost all the time, through their **roots**, from the **soil**.
- Soil is made of tiny particles of rock of different shapes and sizes; the spaces between them are filled with **air and water**.
- **Root hairs** are special cells that grow out of the surface of roots. Each root hair is **part of a single cell**. They give a **really big surface area** for absorbing water and mineral ions.
- Water moves into the root hair cell from the soil: **soil → cell wall → cell membrane → cytoplasm / vacuole**.
- **Minerals** (e.g. **magnesium** and **nitrate**) move into the root hair cell along with the water, as **ions dissolved** in the soil water.
- **Root hair cell parts:** cell wall · cell membrane · cytoplasm · nucleus · **vacuole containing cell sap**.

:::key
**Root hair cells are specialised:** they are **long and thin**, which **increases the surface area** to maximise water and mineral absorption from the soil.
:::

**Parts of a root hair cell (Keynote project)**
| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| **Cell wall** | Structural support and shape; protects the cell from damage |
| **Cell membrane** | Regulates the movement of substances in and out |
| **Cytoplasm** | Fills the cell, enabling transport |
| **Nucleus** | Controls cellular activities |
| **Vacuole** | Stores water and nutrients; keeps the cell firm (turgid) |

## How water moves up the plant — xylem
- In a cross-section of a root you see root hairs on the outside. **In the centre** there are special cells called **xylem vessels** — the **water transport system** of the plant.
- Water moves from the root hair cell **across the root towards the centre**, into the xylem vessels.
- **Xylem vessels** are long, tube-like cells. **They are dead cells** — all their contents (cytoplasm, nucleus) have disappeared; only the **cell walls** remain, with an empty space inside. Their **end walls** have disappeared too, so they stack into **long, empty tubes** from the roots to the top of the plant.
- The **wood** in a tree trunk is made of xylem vessels. A xylem vessel is only about **0.05 mm** across.
- **Root:** xylem in a **cross shape in the centre**. **Stem:** xylem in **bundles arranged in a ring** near the outside.
- **Root cap:** protects the tip of the root as it grows through the soil.

## Keynote project — Plant transport
- **Water absorption by roots:** root hairs absorb water from the soil, starting its journey.
- **Water transport through xylem:** from roots up to the leaves.
- **Celery experiment:** coloured water shows water moving up through the xylem.
- **Xylem cells:** dead, hollow tubes like pipes; in roots, stems and leaves; make up the woody parts of trees.
- **Palisade cells:** just beneath the leaf surface; **tall, column-shaped, packed with chloroplasts**; do photosynthesis (sunlight + water + CO₂ → food).
- **Stoma:** a tiny pore on the leaf surface formed by **two guard cells** that open and close; allows gas exchange (CO₂ in, O₂ out); controls **water loss** by transpiration.
