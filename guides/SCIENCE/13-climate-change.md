# Climate change

**Key words:** slush · mass extinction · meteorites · meteoroids · meteors

## Greenhouse gases
- **Carbon dioxide** and **methane** are **greenhouse gases**: they trap heat energy and keep the Earth warm. Without any CO₂ in the atmosphere the Earth would be **frozen**.
- How it works (Getting started diagram): energy from the Sun passes through the atmosphere and warms the ground → some energy is **reflected** by the ground → some of this reflected energy passes back out and is lost to space → some is **blocked by CO₂** and stays close to the Earth.
- Now we have **too much** CO₂: fossil fuels took millions of years to form and stored carbon underground; **burning** them releases it quickly as CO₂. CO₂ levels are **increasing**, and this affects the climate.

## Climate change in the past
**Climate** = the long-term pattern of temperatures, wind and rainfall.

| When | Event | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **~2 billion years ago** | **First ice age** | Very cold; since then, cycles of warm periods (no ice) and cold **ice ages** (ice at the poles) |
| **~650 million years ago** | **Snowball Earth** ("slushball Earth") | Earth covered in ice and snow (**slush** = melting ice) |
| **~470 million years ago** | **Two asteroids collided** in space (between Earth and Mars) | Huge amounts of **dust** → less light and heat → **ice age**, ice caps spread, sea level fell |
| **~67 million years ago** | **Asteroid hit Earth** (off the coast of Mexico) | Shock waves, high temperatures, **tsunami**; dust in the air → less light → plants couldn't photosynthesise, food chains disrupted, much colder → **dinosaurs died**; **mass extinction** of up to **¾ (75 %)** of species |
| **Today** | Earth **getting hotter** | **More CO₂** |

- There are about **175 known asteroid craters**. A crater in the USA is over **1 km wide and 170 m deep**, from about **50 000 years ago**.

## Meteoroids, meteors and meteorites
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Asteroid** | A big rock in space |
| **Meteoroid** | An object in space **smaller** than an asteroid |
| **Meteor** | A meteoroid that enters Earth's atmosphere and **burns** ("shooting star") |
| **Meteorite** | The part of a meteoroid that **hits the ground** (about 500 a year) |

- In **2013** a meteor exploded over **Russia** and shattered windows. Since **1998**, scientists track objects that could hit Earth, looking at their **mass/diameter** and **closest approach**. Asteroid **2006 DP14** (200 m × 400 m) passed 2 400 000 km from Earth in 2014.

## Climate change today and in the future
- For the last ~2000 years the climate was fairly stable. Now the **mean temperature is increasing**, caused by **more CO₂** (a greenhouse gas). The graph (1880–2016) shows the temperature about **1 °C higher** in 2016 than in 1880 (the teacher's diagram says about **+1.2 °C** today).

:::key
**We burn fuel → the Earth gets hotter → problems**
We burn fuel (oil, coal, gas) **so** there is more CO₂ in the air (a thicker blanket) **so** the Earth gets hotter (about +1.2 °C) **so**:
- **Big storms** — more and stronger (extreme weather);
- **No rain or too much** — droughts, fires, floods;
- **The sea goes up** — ice melts, cities flood (sea level rise).
:::

## Vocabulary
| Word | Definition |
|---|---|
| climate | the usual weather in a place for many years |
| carbon dioxide (CO₂) | a gas in the air; it traps heat |
| greenhouse gas | a gas that keeps the Earth warm |
| fossil fuels | coal, oil and gas from the ground |
| ice age | a very cold time on Earth |
| asteroid | a big rock in space |
| meteor / meteorite | a small space rock that burns in the sky / that hits the ground |
| mass extinction | when many kinds of animals and plants die out |
| sea level rise | the sea gets higher |
| extreme weather | very strong storms, floods or heat |
| drought | a long time with no rain |
| slush | ice that is melting |
