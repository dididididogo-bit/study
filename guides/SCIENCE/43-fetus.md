# Keeping a fetus healthy

**Key words:** fetal · fetus

**Lesson 15–16 aim:** discuss how fetal development is affected by the health of the mother, including **smoking and drugs**.

## A healthy pregnancy
- For the first nine months of its life, a human is a **fetus** growing inside its mother (in the uterus). It depends on the mother for everything it needs.
- Substances **from the mother's blood to the fetus**: **oxygen, nutrients, vitamins, minerals, water**.
- Substances **from the fetus to the mother's blood** (excreted): **carbon dioxide and urea**.

## Diet
A pregnant woman needs a **balanced diet** with enough **protein** (to build the fetus's cells), **carbohydrates** (energy), **calcium**, **iron** and vitamins.
| Vitamin | Why it's needed in pregnancy (teacher's notes) |
|---|---|
| **Vitamin A** | Good **vision** for mother and fetus, and **white blood cells** to fight pathogens |
| **Vitamin C** | Strong **skin and blood vessels** for mother and fetus |
| **Vitamin D** | Strong **bones and teeth** for mother and fetus |

## Things to avoid
- **Smoking:** harmful substances (e.g. carbon monoxide, nicotine) reach the fetus → less oxygen, smaller babies, more risk of problems.
- **Alcohol** — a pregnant woman should avoid alcohol.
- **Illegal drugs** — should never be taken; medicines only after checking with a doctor or pharmacist.
- Too much **caffeine** (coffee, cola) may also harm the fetus.

## Project — how scientists linked smoking to health
Scientists cannot do experiments that make people smoke, so they **collect information** from very large numbers of people and look for **correlations** (e.g. smokers vs non-smokers, health of babies of mothers who smoked).
