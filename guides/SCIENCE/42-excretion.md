# Excretion in humans

**Key words:** bladder · excretion · excretory system · kidneys · renal · urea · ureter · urethra · urine

## What is excretion?
- **Excretion** = getting rid of **waste materials made inside the body** (teacher's notes: *removing waste from the body*).
- **Faeces are not excretion**: food in the digestive tube never really became part of the body's cells.
- Substances humans excrete include:
  - **carbon dioxide** — made in **respiration** (breathed out by the lungs);
  - **urea** — a waste substance made in **liver cells**;
  - **excess water** not needed by the body.

## Excreting urea
- Proteins in food are broken down into small molecules and go into the blood to the **liver**.
- If we have more protein than we need, the liver changes the extra into **urea**, a **poisonous** substance (it makes us ill if it builds up).
- Urea is taken away from the liver in the blood and removed by the **kidneys** in the **excretory system** (also called the **renal system** — "renal" means "to do with kidneys").

:::key
**Urea** — waste from protein, **made in the liver**, **removed by the kidneys in urine**.
**Urine** — liquid waste that leaves the body (urea dissolved in water).
:::

## The urinary (excretory) system
| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| **Renal artery** | Carries **oxygenated blood to** the kidneys (brings blood with urea) |
| **Kidney** | **Filters the blood**, removes all urea and excess water; regulates water content |
| **Renal vein** | Carries **deoxygenated blood away from** the kidneys |
| **Ureter** | Carries **urine from the kidneys to the bladder** |
| **Bladder** | **Stores** urine |
| **Urethra** | Carries urine **to the outside** of the body |

- As blood flows through the kidneys, they **filter** it, removing urea and excess water → **urine** → ureter → bladder → urethra → outside.

:::tip
**Don't mix up the "u" words:** **urea** (the substance) vs **urine** (the liquid); **ureter** (kidney → bladder, two of them) vs **urethra** (bladder → outside, one).
:::
